# Account Deletion Request

**App:** Qaafiyah Expert
**Developer:** Shakeeb Ahmad (Sarbakaf Group)

## Request Account Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your account and the data associated with it, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

**Email:** admin@sarbakaf.com or sarbakafgroup@gmail.com

**Subject:** Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address you use to sign in to the app (Google Sign-In email) so we can identify your account.

---

## What happens when you request deletion?

After verifying your request, we will permanently delete:

- Your account
- Your profile information
- Your app preferences and settings
- Any user-generated content associated with your account
- Any other data stored specifically for your account

---

## Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period if required:

- To comply with legal obligations
- To prevent fraud or abuse
- For security, backup, or audit purposes

Such retained data is only kept for as long as required by applicable laws or legitimate business purposes.

---

## Processing Time

We aim to process account deletion requests within **30 days** of receiving and verifying the request.

Once your account has been deleted, the action is permanent and cannot be undone.
